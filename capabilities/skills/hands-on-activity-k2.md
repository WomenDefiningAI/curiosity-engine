# K–2 hands-on activity

Make it startable in about two minutes with paper, writing utensils, or ordinary household objects. Use a story hook or role, a visible goal, and a repeatable play/experiment loop where the child makes choices and notices consequences. Include material substitutions, concise safety/stop guidance, cleanup, and at least one variation. Do not rename a list of instructions as a game; give the child agency and something observable to discover.
