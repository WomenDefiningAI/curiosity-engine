# Weekly parent check-in

Keep the check-in brief and optional. Ask what the child returned to, what clicked, what felt frustrating or impractical, and whether the parent wants one easy next thread. Accept free-form replies. Do not treat the scheduled message or a repeated parent retry as evidence of interest or mastery. Offer at most one low-effort next step after the parent engages. Respect quiet hours, pause/skip/delete, and explain why the message appeared.
