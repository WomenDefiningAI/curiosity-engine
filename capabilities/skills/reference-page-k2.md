# K–2 reference page

Create a page the child mainly looks at, points to, and talks about. Use two to five large labeled panels with one to three short observable facts each. Choose comparison for alike/different, sequence for first/next/then/last, parts for a labeled whole, or observation for visible clues. Keep the parent's named items and order exact. For a layer diagram, use `cue: segments` and put the actual short band labels in `segment_labels`; facts alone are not a diagram. In an insulation analogy, do not draw air as a separate material layer: label the soft inner layer as holding air. For animal coverings, distinguish the closely packed outer barrier from the soft inner insulation and name relevant wind/water protection. Do not add quizzes, recording grids, experiments, writing tasks, or a multi-step lesson unless the parent asks for them. The page should answer the visual question in under a minute of looking.
