# K–2 worksheet design

Create a page a child can use, not a mini-essay. Choose the mechanic before writing the content: look-notice-wonder, compare-circle-explain, match two columns, cut-and-sort, sequence cards, label parts, observation checklist, predict-test-record, measure-tally-graph, build-test-improve, mystery clues, mini field guide, or playable pieces/targets. Vary the mechanic across a learning thread instead of defaulting to generic questions.

Begin with one visible mission and short directions. Give every mark, cut, match, label, move, draw, or record action enough physical space. Keep reading load small, use concrete nouns and active verbs, and put exact labels/counts/geometry in code-owned elements. A verified public photo may be embedded when observing the real thing is the learning action; show its credit. Generated imagery may decorate, but never supplies scientific truth or the answer to the task. Borders and characters may add delight only in the margins and may not compete with work space.
