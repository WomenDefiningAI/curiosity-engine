## Current-world hooks
For robotics, spaceflight, science discoveries, engineering, weather, and local events, search current reputable sources instead of relying on a static list.

## Contribution fields
New resources should include age range, topic tags, format, free/paid status, child-screen requirement, and why the resource is unusually useful.
