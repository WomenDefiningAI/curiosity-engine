# Roadmap

## Current status

The Phase 0–6 software candidate now includes parent-visible/correctable learner context, typed pedagogy and evidence,
evaluated knowledge-bearing artifacts, governed adaptation, shadow personalization, and a closed advisory research
track. A frozen preservation commit retains its original evidence. The beta now uses a trusted-parent threat model:
ordinary help is lightweight, child-usable material gets focused quality/safety checks and parent review, and only
durable or external action receives the strongest consent/provenance machinery.

The active Lab is 91 cases rather than 1,915. Its five focused safety cases are outweighed by seven executable
family-usefulness cases and six performance/coherence cases. The default pytest gate is 259 representative cases
rather than 3,754, with a hard ceiling of 275; exhaustive phase, routing, capability, Slack, artifact, and visual
matrices remain explicitly runnable outside default discovery.
This is intentional scope reduction, not loss of the audit trail.

## Next validation and operations

- conduct consented parent/child co-design sessions and resolve observed comprehension, accessibility, and
  emotional-safety issues;
- validate the ordinary parent-assistance flow without Phase 5/6 promotion machinery in its critical path;
- keep a successful quick answer to one plan, one generation, and one combined usefulness review; keep quick answers
  text-first unless the parent explicitly asks for a visual;
- let exploratory compare/test/observe/sequence questions produce one optional kid-friendly learning diagram without
  an extra completion call; use image generation to make diagrams and worksheets engaging, never as a substitute for
  their learning structure;
- keep personalization shadow-only and research disabled until a concrete family trial justifies them;
- convert recurring parent feedback into de-identified regression proposals only after privacy/licensing review;
- broaden factual, age-fit, diagram/worksheet usefulness, OCR, visual-QA, image-edit, latency, and cost evaluation;
- retire redundant authorization hashes, external high-water/multi-root authority, and process-kill permutations in
  measured compatibility slices while retaining consent, provenance, deletion, restart, and rollback coverage;
- add macOS always-on service support and smoother upgrade/rollback commands.

## Later integrations

- Telegram through the existing transport contract;
- parent-authorized school email and calendar context;
- conventional-printer discovery and queues;
- hosted or multi-parent operation under a separate threat model.

Every integration needs explicit consent, least privilege, provenance, expiry, and tests.

## Further context research

Possible later work includes embedding-assisted retrieval and an optional graph database. The current candidate already
has conservative evidence-derived projections, fixed-Beta advisory uncertainty, and evidence-gated proactive
candidates; they remain shadow/non-deliverable until their explicit gates pass. Future research must preserve
uncertainty, separate retries from independent episodes, and keep `do_nothing` valid.

## Out of scope

WhatsApp, child accounts, autonomous purchasing, automatic campaigns or release promotion, and specialized
fabrication are outside the current plan.
